# Value Communication

> **Status: In Development** - Core templates captured; audience framing guidance pending.

## Purpose

This skill helps translate outcome-oriented thinking into compelling value communication for different audiences. It bridges the gap between defining outcomes (what we measure) and communicating value (why it matters).

**Key distinction:**
- **Value Proposition** = Customer-focused statement of why someone should use a product/service
- **Positioning Statement** = Internal tool for market position and differentiation

## Core Concept: Customer Segment + Value Map

Effective value communication requires understanding both sides:

**Customer Segment** (understand their world):
- **Customer Jobs** - What they're trying to accomplish
- **Pains** - What frustrates them
- **Gains** - What outcomes they want

**Value Map** (match your offering):
- **Products & Services** - What you offer
- **Pain Relievers** - How you reduce frustration
- **Gain Creators** - How you deliver outcomes

## Value Proposition Templates

Eight templates for different contexts (full details in `references/value-proposition-templates.md`):

| Template | Format | Best For |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Benefit-oriented | "For [target] who [need], our [product] is a [category] that [benefit]" | New product launches |
| Pain-point focused | "We help [audience] [solve pain] by [mechanism]" | Known frustrations |
| Outcome-based | "We help [audience] achieve [outcome] through [approach]" | Outcome-driven orgs |
| Emotional appeal | "Experience [emotive benefit] with our [product], designed for [audience]" | UX-focused products |
| Convenience-driven | "We make [task] easier for [audience] through [feature]" | Efficiency improvements |
| Testimonial-based | "See why [number] [audience] trust our [product] for [goal]" | Established products |
| Cost-efficiency | "Save [time/money] and achieve [goal] with our [product]" | Budget-conscious buyers |
| Risk reduction | "With our [product], reduce the risk of [problem] in [scenario]" | Risk-averse audiences |

## Positioning Statement

For internal strategic alignment:

```
For [target audience] who [need], our [product name] is a [category] that [differentiating benefit]. Unlike [competitors], our [product] [primary differentiation].
```

## Integration with Outcome-Oriented Thinking

Value propositions flow from outcome work:

| Outcome Framework | Provides |
|-------------------|----------|
| Problem Statement | Pain point for pain-focused templates |
| Impact Mapping | Target audience and desired outcomes |
| Hypothesis | Mechanism (how we solve it) |
| Metrics | Quantification for cost/testimonial templates |

## Audience-Specific Framing (Planned)

Different audiences need different emphasis:

| Audience | Focus On | Avoid |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Executives | Mission impact, strategic alignment, ROI | Technical implementation details |
| Engineers | Problem context, success criteria, constraints | Business jargon, vague benefits |
| Customers | Their pain, their gain, ease of use | Internal metrics, org structure |
| Procurement | Compliance, risk reduction, total cost | Feature lists without outcomes |
