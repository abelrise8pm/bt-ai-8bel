**Defining Gap Period Anti-Goals**
The team identified anti-goals to maintain focus during the 2 to 5 week contract gap. These guidelines prioritize efficiency and prevent scope creep by discouraging premature solution development.

**Goal Synthesis and Prioritization**
Participants synthesized goals into core themes including user validation and mission operating system fundamentals. It was decided that all project bets must function as experiments with defined outcomes.

**Framework and Task Alignment**
The team aligned on project management frameworks and prioritized onboarding and design system investigation. A collaborative decision was made to utilize experimental, hypothesis-driven design for upcoming tasks.

### DECISIONS (Aligned)

* **Anti-goals established for contract gap** The team establishes a set of anti-goals for the contract gap, including avoiding overwork, preventing solution-first development without user validation, not ignoring outcomes, and prioritizing team cohesion.

* **Prioritization of lightweight, actionable work** The team adopts a strategy to prioritize lightweight, actionable tasks during the contract gap to ensure progress is made even if the gap duration is short.

* **Team norming requirement for new hires** The team will conduct a team norming session every time a new member joins to ensure culture and consistency.

* **Definition standard for project bets** Every project bet is to be defined as an experiment, including a hypothesis, baseline metrics, a persona, and a specific pain point.

# ANTI-GOALS AND GOALS

### Anti-Goals

During the TAK Beach Gap Period, we will not:
- Lock ourselves into a direction for the future vision based on assumptions we can't validate yet.
- Move into the OTA contract without drafting problem statements and exploring target mission-outcomes for the future vision.
- Do throw-away work by building things without grounding in user input, problem evidence, or feasibility.
- Burn out before OTA begins by spreading thin across beach projects and not taking time to know each other.


### "OTA gap" Project Goals

During the TAK Beach Gap Period (May 20 - June 15), we will:
- Establish the Mission OS foundation for OTA by first week of June, with the current state defined, the target condition identified, and an assumption validation cycle established.
- Within a week, align the whole team on shared project context, the future vision (SOAP), and the proposed bets.
- By June 8, ready the OTA project setup: required CAC/access docs submitted with POCs identified, backlog consolidated to a single project board, and a current-state path-to-prod artifact in place.
- Establish and iterate on team operating norms by June 15, including pairing across the team and cross-role AI coordination.
- Build evidence-based prototypes in weekly increments to remain flexible with our learnings towards the OTA.
