# Value Proposition & Positioning Templates

> **Source:** Extracted from Elev8R prompt (Gemini Gem for outcome-oriented thinking training)

## Overview

A value proposition is a customer-focused statement that articulates why someone should use a product or service. It should be crafted based on the core theme and finalized before any launch, regardless of scale.

A positioning statement is an internal tool that outlines brand position in the market and how the product differs from alternatives. It guides marketing and business decisions.

**Key distinction:**
- **Value Proposition** = Customer-focused (external communication)
- **Positioning Statement** = Market position (internal alignment)

## Value Proposition Components

### Customer Segment Analysis

Understand what customers are trying to do and their pains and gains:

| Component | Definition | Questions to Answer |
|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Customer Jobs** | What the customer is trying to get done | What practical tasks? What emotional needs? What social goals? |
| **Pains** | What frustrates customers while performing jobs | What makes them frustrated? What risks do they face? What obstacles exist? |
| **Gains** | What outcomes and benefits customers want | What would delight them? What would make their job easier? What do they aspire to? |

### Value Map

Match offerings to customer needs:

| Component | Definition | Questions to Answer |
|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Products & Services** | What you offer that helps customers complete jobs | What do we provide? How does it help them accomplish their goals? |
| **Pain Relievers** | How offerings alleviate customer pains | Which pains do we eliminate? How do we reduce frustration? |
| **Gain Creators** | How offerings improve customer outcomes | Which gains do we create? How do we exceed expectations? |

## Value Proposition Templates

Use these templates to articulate product/feature value. Replace bracketed placeholders with specific content.

### 1. Benefit-Oriented

```
For [target customer] who [need/want], our [product/service] is a [product category] that [statement of benefit].
```

**Example:**
```
For veterans applying for disability benefits who want faster claim decisions, our pre-submission checklist is a guidance tool that ensures complete documentation on first submission.
```

### 2. Pain-Point Focused

```
We help [target audience] [solve a specific pain point] by [product feature or benefit].
```

**Example:**
```
We help claims processors reduce evidence request overhead by automatically identifying missing documentation before submission.
```

### 3. Outcome-Based

```
We help [target audience] achieve [desired outcome] through our [unique approach].
```

**Example:**
```
We help development teams achieve 90%+ deployment success rates through our guided deployment wizard with real-time validation.
```

### 4. Emotional Appeal

```
Experience [emotive benefit] with our [product/service], designed for [target audience].
```

**Example:**
```
Experience peace of mind with our claims tracking dashboard, designed for veterans waiting for benefit decisions.
```

### 5. Convenience-Driven

```
We make [task or goal] easier and more efficient for [target audience] through [specific feature or benefit].
```

**Example:**
```
We make appointment scheduling easier and more efficient for patients through 24/7 self-service booking with automatic provider matching.
```

### 6. Testimonial-Based

```
See why [number] [target audience] trust our [product/service] for [specific problem or goal].
```

**Example:**
```
See why 50,000 small businesses trust our SAM registration assistant for navigating federal contracting requirements.
```

### 7. Cost-Efficiency

```
Save [time/money/resources] and achieve [solution/goal] with our [product/service].
```

**Example:**
```
Save 40 hours per month and achieve complete audit compliance with our automated evidence collection system.
```

### 8. Risk Reduction

```
With our [product/service], reduce the risk of [specific problem or issue] in [specific scenario].
```

**Example:**
```
With our pre-submission validator, reduce the risk of claim denial due to missing evidence in disability benefit applications.
```

## Positioning Statement Template

Use for internal alignment on market position:

```
For [target audience] who [target audience need], our [product or service name] is a [product or service category] that [statement of differentiating benefit]. Unlike [competitors], our [product or service name] [statement of primary differentiation].
```

**Example:**
```
For federal agencies who need to modernize legacy systems, our platform migration toolkit is an infrastructure modernization solution that reduces migration risk through incremental validation. Unlike big-bang migration approaches, our toolkit enables continuous operation during transition with rollback capability at every stage.
```

## Template Selection Guide

| Situation | Recommended Template |
|-----------|---------------------|
| New product launch | Benefit-oriented or Outcome-based |
| Addressing known frustration | Pain-point focused |
| Competing on user experience | Emotional appeal or Convenience-driven |
| Established product with traction | Testimonial-based |
| Budget-conscious audience | Cost-efficiency |
| Risk-averse audience (government, healthcare) | Risk reduction |
| Internal strategic alignment | Positioning statement |

## Integration with Outcome-Oriented Thinking

Value propositions should flow from outcome-oriented work:

1. **Problem Statement** → Identifies the pain point for pain-focused templates
2. **Impact Mapping** → Identifies target audience and desired outcomes
3. **Hypothesis** → Provides the mechanism (how we solve it) for templates
4. **Metrics** → Provides quantification for cost-efficiency and testimonial templates

**Example flow:**

```
Problem: Veterans submit incomplete claims, causing 60% evidence request rate

Hypothesis: Pre-submission checklist will drive 75%+ complete submissions

Value Proposition (Pain-focused):
"We help veterans avoid claim delays by showing exactly which evidence
is required before submission."

Value Proposition (Outcome-based):
"We help veterans achieve faster benefit decisions through our
evidence completeness checklist that validates documentation upfront."
```

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing a value proposition:

- [ ] Target audience is specific (not "everyone" or "users")
- [ ] Benefit is concrete and measurable where possible
- [ ] Language matches audience vocabulary (no jargon for customers)
- [ ] Differentiator is defensible (competitors can't easily claim the same)
- [ ] Connects to validated problem/outcome from research
- [ ] Passes the "so what?" test (audience cares about this benefit)
